THE TENTS

Check out the pros, cons, and youtube reviews.

01 The comfort pick

REI Half Dome 2+

Room to stretch out

A good, roomy tent for weekend adventures. Your good ol familiar favorite. Not the smallest to carry, but nice to have a little extra space for Cecil.

+ PROS

  • More room to spread out
  • A good fit for regular camping trips
  • Space for our stuff + Cecil

− CONS

  • Heavier to carry
  • Takes up a bit more room if backpacking

02 The lightweight pick

NEMO Hornet 2P

Go farther, carry less

Lightweight little baby adventure tent. Great for a backpacking situation, though we might get pretty cozy sharing it with Cecil.

+ PROS

  • Very light to carry
  • Packs down small
  • Great for backpacking

− CONS

  • A little snug for two people and a dawg
  • Less room for bags and other gear

03 The bikepacking pick

MSR Hubba Hubba Bikepack

Made for two wheels

I know how you love biking so thought this one might tickle your fancy. It attaches to your bike! A fun pick if you want to ride somewhere beautiful or go on a bike-marathon.

+ PROS

  • Packs neatly for bike trips
  • Lightweight for its style
  • Cool af

− CONS

  • Best suited to bikepacking
  • Still a cozy space for 2.5 and all our stuff
▶ WATCH REVIEWS ↗ ↗ MSR WEBSITE
04 The premium pick

Big Agnes Copper Spur 2P

Lightweight meets comfort

Lightweight, comfy and fancy. For adventures where we want to bring less but still sleep well.

+ PROS

  • Light to carry
  • Nice balance of weight and space
  • Two doors make getting in and out easier

− CONS

  • Lightweight fabric needs a little care
  • Not as roomy as a bigger tent, but could fit us and Cecil okay
▶ WATCH REVIEWS ↗ ↗ BIG AGNES WEBSITE
05 The extra-space pick

Big Agnes Tiger Wall 3P

Room for more possibilities

More space for our outdoor sleepovers. Cuddle with Cecil, hide from bugs together. Not as lightweight as the 2P, but about the same as the Half Dome.

+ PROS

  • Extra space for two people
  • Still designed with backpacking in mind
  • High quality materials and TWO DOORS

− CONS

  • A bit bigger to pack than a two-person tent
  • Needs to be staked out properly
